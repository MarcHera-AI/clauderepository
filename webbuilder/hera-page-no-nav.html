FAQ

Common Questions

What exactly does Hera Solutions do?

We run Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ad campaigns for contractors and turn them into qualified homeowner appointments. We handle the ads, the creative, the lead screening and the booking. You handle the quote and the job.

Are the leads exclusive to my business?

Yes. Every lead we generate is yours alone. We never resell or share leads between clients, and we don't work with two competing contractors in the same service area.

Which trades do you work with?

We work with deck builders and contractors across the US and Canada. Our system is built to scale in any local market - urban, suburban, or rural.

How quickly will I see leads?

Campaigns typically go live within 48 hours of onboarding, and most clients see their first qualified appointments within the first week or two while the campaigns dial in.

What makes you different from other agencies?

Simple: we don't just send you leads. We qualify them by phone, automate follow-up, and book real estimates. We're measured on revenue impact - not impressions or clicks.
